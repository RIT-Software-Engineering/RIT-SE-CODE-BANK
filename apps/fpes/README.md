# FPES Portal Application

The point of this application is to create a single portal allowing faculty members to submit various forms for evaluating their performance over the academic/calendar year. There are three different forms that are able to be filled out by users: Highlights Form, Plan of Work Form, and the Appraisal Form.

## User Roles

There are currently 3 user roles in this project. All 3 have their own explicit privileges, and one user (as of right now) can have multiple roles depending on the permissions given to their account.

### Faculty Member
    Faculty Members are typically professors. They have the ability and the obligation to fill out both the Highlights and the Plan of Work (PoW) forms. They are only able to see their own forms they have filled out except for appraisal forms filled out by their supervisor. Faculty members are required to sign off on these submitted appraisal forms as well as respond whether they believe the appraisal form accurately grades their achievement.

### Supervisor
    Supervisors are assigned Faculty Members who they are able to review and sign off on those Faculty Members Highlights and PoW Forms. They also are expected to complete Appraisal Forms for each other there assigned faculty members. Supervisors currently can also be Faculty Members depending on whether or not they are assigned that role.

### Admin
    The Admin role allows the user to make changes to user permissions and faculty information. They are also able to make changes to the information that cannot be changed by normal users such as the list of courses that can be selected by faculty members when adding course sections.

## Types of Forms

### Highlights Form
    The Highlights Form is meant to be filled out by Faculty Members at the end of the calendar year. The form mainly comprises that faculty members various accomplishments and duties over that year including courses taught, grants they have worked on, publications submitted, and other internal or external service.

### Plan of Work (PoW) Form
    The PoW Form is meant to be filled out by Faculty Members at the end of the scholastic year (before Fall semester). The PoW Form mainly encompasses the Faculty Members upcoming course load.
